# Readouts

Dated evidence between [[raw/_README|raw sources]] and durable concepts. See [[SCHEMA]] for the contract; use `templates/readout.md`.

Record context, observations, analysis, decisions, verification, and limitations. Use for experiments, incidents, diagnostics, reports, and before/after comparisons. Extract durable insights into concepts and retain the readout link.

Curated readouts may be indexed when useful. Generated factcheck batches/summaries are not individually indexed and are exempt from ordinary minimum-link/orphan requirements; frontmatter, provenance, and resolvable wikilinks still matter.
