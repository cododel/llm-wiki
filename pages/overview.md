# Wiki overview

This is an empty knowledge base with reusable tooling. Choose the domain in [[SCHEMA]] before ingesting material. The human chooses what to preserve and investigate; agents help structure, connect, and maintain it within [[AGENTS]].

Raw sources preserve input. Readouts record dated observations. Concepts synthesize durable knowledge. Ideas and notes remain provisional; drafts preserve original voice. Wikilinks connect interpretations without replacing provenance.

Use [[pages/workflows]] for operations and [[index]] for the curated catalog. [[pages/open-questions]] holds unresolved process decisions, not a copied backlog from another owner.
